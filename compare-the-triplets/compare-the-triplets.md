# Compare the Triplets

## Problem
Compare the scores of Alice and Bob element by element and calculate their points.

## HackerRank
- Platform: HackerRank
- Difficulty: Easy
- Language: C++11

## Approach
1. Compare Alice's and Bob's scores at each of the three positions.
2. Give one point to Alice if her score is higher.
3. Give one point to Bob if his score is higher.
4. Return both scores.

## Time Complexity
O(1)

## Space Complexity
O(1)
